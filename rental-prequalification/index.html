How the low-debt review works

Standard pathway / possible LD pathway: When two results are shown, the first remains the standard pathway. The second is available only after the low-debt and other applicable requirements are verified.

Income of at least 2.25× rent

  • Rent plus verified required monthly debt generally must not exceed 45% of verified gross monthly income.
  • Minimum post-move-in reserves generally range from one month of rent at 3.00× income or higher to three months at 2.25×–2.49×.
  • Stable income, satisfactory rental history, no material unpaid landlord debt, application accuracy, and any required guaranty or payment protection must still be verified.

Income of 2.00×–2.24× rent

  • Non-rent debt generally must not exceed 5% of gross monthly income.
  • Rent plus verified required debt generally must not exceed 50% of gross monthly income.
  • Six months of rent must generally remain in qualifying reserves after move-in.
  • Remaining income after rent, utilities, and debt must generally equal at least the greater of $1,750 or 75% of monthly rent.

Lower credit scores

LD options may exist in some 560–599 and 520–559 cells, but they remain credit or combined-risk reviews. Low debt does not cure unpaid landlord debt, active rental collections, serious recent housing defaults, fraud, altered documents, or a recent pattern of material nonpayment. No routine LD pathway is published below 520.
